Quality Improvement (QI)

Quality Improvement (QI) is about working together to make our services better for everyone, one small step at a time. Quality Improvement is not just a way of doing things it’s about making our work better, safer, and easier so that the people who use our services have an improved experience

Not all learning happens in a classroom.

Learning half day’s are a short block of time where teams stop their normal work so they can learn, think, and plan small changes together.

Trust-wide Contest Closing: 01.09.2026

QI Posters: Competition Time!

Create your team’s QI poster for this year’s contest. Top prize worth £5,000.

As part of this year’s trust‑wide Quality Improvement (QI) contest, each team is asked to create a QI poster showcasing an audit, research project, or improvement initiative that has taken place, or is currently underway, since 1 January 2025.

During your learning half days, you are invited to spend dedicated time working together to start building your poster.

Step-by-Step Activity:
  • Meet as a group in Teams
  • Choose the QI project, idea or improvement you want to showcase
  • Gather key information and data
  • Agree your main messages about the impact you’ve made
  • Start creating your draft poster ready for submission
Why this is important:
  • Highlight great work happening in your team
  • Celebrate improvements made for colleagues, patients or services
  • Share learning across the trust and contribute to a trust‑wide event
What you need:
  • Your Learning Half Day time
